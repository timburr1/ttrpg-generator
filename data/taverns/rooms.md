# The room tonight

- Packed and roaring. A festival, a payday, or a wedding. Getting a drink takes ten minutes.
- Nearly empty. A single drunk sleeps by the fire.
- Tense and quiet. Two groups sit on opposite sides of the room, watching each other.
- Mourning. Black ribbons on the tables; a regular died yesterday.
- A music night. A local band is loud, enthusiastic, and not good.
- Smoky and hot. The chimney is blocked and nobody has fixed it.
- Gambling night. Every table has dice or cards, and the keeper takes a share.
- Storm outside. Everyone is stuck here, and strangers are getting to know each other.
- Suspiciously friendly. Everyone is welcoming, generous, and a little too interested in the party.
- Cleaning up after last night. Broken chairs, a hole in the wall, and nobody wants to talk about it.
