# Innkeepers

- Old Marga, tiny and ancient, who throws out anyone she doesn't like with no apparent effort.
- Bastian Hale, a retired soldier who keeps his sword behind the bar and mentions it often.
- The twins, Odo and Ida, who finish each other's sentences and run the place in shifts. Nobody has seen them in the same room.
- Wenna Crale, a cheerful widow who has buried three husbands and is courting a fourth.
- Fat Jory, a former smuggler who still knows every back road and every guard's price.
- Sister Pell, an ex-priestess who left her order and won't say why. Blesses every keg.
- Tamsin Rook, barely twenty, who inherited the place last month and is in over her head.
- Grubb, a huge, silent cook-owner who communicates mostly by pointing with a ladle.
- Lord Ambrose Vell, a disgraced noble who runs a tavern as if it were a manor. Insists on table manners.
- Neddie Quill, a gossip who trades rumors for tips and tips for rumors.
- Mother Agathe, who reads palms for regulars and is unsettlingly accurate.
- Nobody. The previous keeper vanished; a rotating committee of regulars keeps it running out of habit.
