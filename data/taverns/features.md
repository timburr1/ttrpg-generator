# Something odd about the building

- One door upstairs is bricked over. The room count on the guest ledger doesn't add up.
- The floor slopes noticeably. Dropped coins roll to the same corner every time.
- A tree grows through the middle of the common room and out the roof.
- The cellar connects to old tunnels the keeper insists are "just for storage."
- There's a booth no regular will sit in, and the keeper won't seat anyone there.
- The building used to be a temple. A faded altar is now the bar.
- A clock above the fireplace runs backward and has never been wound.
- The back half of the building is new, the front half is very old, and the seam between them is cold to the touch.
